Festivals

Cultural Festivals

Guimaras celebrates life through vibrant fiestas, sacred reenactments, and community festivals rooted in faith and harvest.

May
Manggahan Festival
Jordan (Province-wide)
The province-wide mango festival with street dances, trade fairs, mango competitions, and the famous Mango-All-You-Can challenge. Guimaras' biggest annual celebration.
March/April – Holy Week
Pagtaltal sa Balaan Bukid
Jordan
A dramatic Passion of Christ reenactment on Balaan Bukid. Thousands of pilgrims and tourists witness this powerful display of faith and cultural heritage.
June
Balsahan Festival
Sibunag
Sibunag's river heritage festival featuring colorful cultural performances, local delicacies, and showcasing of the town's natural waterways.
June
Sadsaran Festival
Nueva Valencia
Named after the local word for dancing. This colorful festival celebrates the cultural heritage of Nueva Valencia with traditional dances, songs, and performances.
May
Palayag Festival
Buenavista
'Palayag' means to sail. This festival symbolizes the faith and unity of Buenavista's coastal community, with boat processions and vibrant street dancing.
August
San Lorenzo Fiesta
San Lorenzo
Annual fiesta honoring Saint Lawrence with religious processions, thanksgiving masses, and community feasts highlighting local agricultural produce.

Culture & Traditions

🤝
Bayanihan Spirit

The spirit of community cooperation — "bayanihan" — is alive in Guimaras. Neighbors help each other in farming, fishing, and during celebrations. It is the foundation of the Ilonggo way of life.

⛪
Deep Religious Faith

Religion plays a central role in everyday life. Regular participation in church activities, fiestas, and the annual Pagtaltal reenactment reflects the strong Catholic faith of the Guimarasnon people.

🥭
Mango Farming Culture

Mango farming is not just an industry — it is a way of life and a source of identity. Families have farmed mangoes for generations, and the harvest season is a time of celebration and pride.

🐟
Fishing Traditions

Coastal communities rely on fishing traditions passed down from generation to generation. Traditional methods, communal boat launches, and respect for the sea define the coastal culture of Guimaras.
