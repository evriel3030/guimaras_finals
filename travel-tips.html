Before You Go

Essential Travel Tips

Everything you need to know before visiting the sweetest island province in the Philippines.

🌤️
Best Time to Visit

November to May is the dry season — ideal for beaches and island hopping. Mango season peaks April–June. Avoid typhoon season (July–October) for outdoor trips and boat rides.

⛴️
How to Get There

Take a ferry from Iloilo City's Ortiz or Parola Wharf to Jordan or Buenavista Port. The trip takes 15–25 minutes and costs ₱15–₱30. Ferries run frequently throughout the day.

🛺
Transportation Options

Use habal-habal (motorcycle taxis) to reach most tourist spots. Hire a multicab or jeepney for groups. Book island-hopping boat tours from Jordan or Nueva Valencia ports in advance.

💵
Budget & Cash

ATMs are limited — bring enough Philippine Peso cash. Most small vendors, boat operators, and resorts do not accept cards. Budget ₱800–₱2,000 per person per day for meals, transport, and fees.

🛡️
Safety Reminders

Guimaras is very safe, but always wear a life jacket on boat rides — required by law. Keep valuables secure, apply sunscreen, stay hydrated, and avoid swimming alone in open water.

🌿
Eco-Tourism Rules

Do not touch or collect corals in protected areas like Taklong Island. Pack out all trash — many islands have no waste facilities. Support local by buying from community vendors and cooperatives.

👗
What to Pack

Light breathable clothing, reef-safe sunscreen, insect repellent, water shoes, and a reusable water bottle. Bring modest attire for church and religious site visits. A light rain jacket is useful outside dry season.

📅
Book Ahead for Festivals

Accommodation fills up fast during Manggahan Festival (May) and Holy Week. Book resorts and island-hopping tours 2–3 weeks in advance during these peak periods.

🥭
Mango Tips

Mangoes are sweetest from April to June. Buy directly from local vendors for the freshest options. Try the Mango-All-You-Can challenge at the Manggahan Festival in May!
