Sibunag

Peaceful southernmost municipality with beaches, waterfalls, and eco-island resorts.

  1. Home
  2. Sibunag

Sibunag is a municipality in the province of Guimaras, Philippines. According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 23,787 people. The municipality is the southernmost in Guimaras and is known for its peaceful island culture and natural attractions.
